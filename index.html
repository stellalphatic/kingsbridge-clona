Common questions

Are you licensed immigration consultants?

Yes. Kingsbridge Immigration is represented by Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants (RCICs) in good standing with the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC).

How much does it cost?

Consultations are billed at a flat rate and credited toward your file if you retain us. Full representation is flat-fee per application type — quoted in writing before any work starts.

Can you guarantee approval?

No honest consultant can. What we guarantee is a complete, accurate, on-time application and straight answers about your chances before you spend money.

Do you work with clients outside Canada?

Yes — most of our clients start overseas. Consultations are by video call, and your file is handled entirely online.
